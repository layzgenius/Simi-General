## Competitive Landscape

| App | What it does | Simi's edge |
|-----|-------------|-------------|
| Shazam | Identifies songs playing around you | Completely different — Simi finds songs *like* yours |
| SoundHound | Song ID + lyrics | Same — different use case |
| Spotify Discover Weekly | Algorithmic recommendations based on history | Simi is intent-based (you pick the seed), multi-platform |
| Last.fm | Social listening + scrobble-based recs | Simi works without account history, cross-platform |
| Music Matcher (web) | Similar songs via web | Simi is native iOS, Vibe Map, richer reasons |

**Key insight:** No native iOS app owns "find songs that feel like this one." The search intent exists ("songs like X", "find similar songs", "music vibe") but no app addresses it well. Simi can own this niche.

```

```

```
Simi – Music Discovery by Vibe
```

```
Free. No account. No subscription.

Most music apps recommend based on what other people with similar taste listen to. That's fine for general discovery but terrible for moments. Simi looks at the song itself — its actual audio properties — and finds what genuinely sounds and feels similar.
```

## Screenshot Strategy (6 screens, first 3 are decisive)

Apple's algorithm surfaces the first 3 screenshots in search results before the user taps. These need to tell the full story in 3 frames.

### Screenshot 1 — The Hook
**Headline (overlay text):** "Find songs that feel the same."
**Visual:** Home screen showing the paste field with a Spotify link already entered, "Find Similar Songs" button prominent.
**Sub-caption:** "Paste any link. Spotify · Apple Music · YouTube · SoundCloud"
**Purpose:** Instant clarity on what Simi does.

### Screenshot 2 — The Differentiator (most important)
**Headline:** "See where your song lives."
**Visual:** The Vibe Map with dots clustered around a source track. Energy vs. Mood axes labeled.
**Sub-caption:** "The Vibe Map shows every match, plotted by energy and mood."
**Purpose:** No other app has this. It's visually distinctive in the App Store grid and immediately signals this is something new.

### Screenshot 3 — The Results
**Headline:** "Ranked by how they actually feel."
**Visual:** The results list showing 3-4 song cards with "Near-perfect", "Identical vibe" badges and tags like "Same Mood · Moody & Driving".
**Sub-caption:** "Every recommendation comes with a reason."
**Purpose:** Shows depth — it's not just a list of songs, it explains itself.

### Screenshot 4 — Multi-platform Input
**Headline:** "Works with every platform."
**Visual:** Paste field showing 4 different link formats, or the "Search by Name" tab.
**Sub-caption:** "Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, SoundCloud, or just a song name."
**Purpose:** Removes the "but I don't use Spotify" objection immediately.

### Screenshot 5 — Song Detail
**Headline:** "Know exactly why it matches."
**Visual:** An expanded song card showing BPM, Energy %, Mood %, Danceability %, Acoustic %.
**Sub-caption:** "The audio fingerprint behind every recommendation."
**Purpose:** Appeals to the analytically curious listener who wants to understand the match.

### Screenshot 6 — Recent + Clean UI
**Headline:** "Pick up where you left off."
**Visual:** Home screen with Recent section populated (3-4 past searches).
**Sub-caption:** "Your searches, saved. No account required."
**Purpose:** Shows polish and the "no account" framing as a feature.
